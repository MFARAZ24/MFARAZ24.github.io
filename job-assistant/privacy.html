Privacy Policy

Job Assistant · Last updated November 23, 2026

Who uses this app

Job Assistant is a personal tool used only by its owner, Muhammad Faraz Shoaib. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data it accesses

The app requests only the Google Drive "drive.file" permission. That lets it create and edit the files it creates itself (a job-tracker spreadsheet and application files). It cannot see or change any other files in the owner's Google Drive.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to keep the owner's job-application tracker and files up to date.
  • The app runs on the owner's own computer. Its sign-in token and data are stored there.
  • Files the app creates in Google Drive are private to the owner and are not shared.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's security settings (Third-party apps with account access).
