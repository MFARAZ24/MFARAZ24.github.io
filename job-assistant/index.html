Job Assistant

A private, single-user tool by Muhammad Faraz Shoaib.

Job Assistant is a personal application that runs on its owner's own computer. It helps organize a job search: tracking job postings, preparing résumés and keeping an application tracker.

It uses Google sign-in only to create and update its own tracker spreadsheet and application files in the owner's Google Drive. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
